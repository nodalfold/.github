## Where we begin

Nodalfold is a technology company working at the intersection of media, artificial intelligence and the exploration of new informational methods.

We are interested in the questions that emerge when new tools change how we research, create knowledge and automate work: what can we discover, what can we make more accessible and what deserves better infrastructure.

## Three directions, one curiosity

| | What we work on |
| --- | --- |
| **Media** | New ways to create, organize and distribute experiences and information. |
| **Artificial intelligence** | Systems that expand our ability to analyze, synthesize and act on knowledge. |
| **Research and automation** | Methods and tools that turn complex processes into clearer, faster and repeatable workflows. |

## The Nodalfold way

We do not build technology for its own sake. We prefer to build the missing piece: a small tool when it solves a specific problem, or a platform when it opens a new line of exploration.

- **Build from within.** We create the infrastructure we need to move forward with autonomy.
- **Rigorous exploration.** We turn new ideas into prototypes, experiments and systems that can endure.
- **Information with intent.** We design around how people find, understand and use knowledge.
- **Responsible automation.** We delegate the repeatable so human judgment can focus on what matters.

### [`commit-curation`](https://github.com/nodalfold/commit-curation)

A TypeScript library and CLI for applying configurable policies to Git commits. It helps repositories maintain a shared language, manageable changes and verifiable rules locally or in CI.

Learn more:

- [README.md](https://github.com/nodalfold/commit-curation/blob/main/README.md) — overview and getting started.

- [CHEATSHEET.md](https://github.com/nodalfold/commit-curation/blob/main/CHEATSHEET.md) — quick reference for commands and profiles.
